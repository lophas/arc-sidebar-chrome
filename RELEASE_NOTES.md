## Arc Side of the Chrome 4.0.5

*Spaces, tabs & a little harmony.*

- Prevent the Autohide sidebar from briefly flashing into view during page loading, reloading or a change of sidebar side. Normal hover opening and closing animations are preserved.

For an unpacked installation, download `arc-side-of-the-chrome-v4.0.5.zip`, replace the files in your existing folder and click **Reload** at `chrome://extensions`.
For a Chrome Web Store upload, use `arc-side-of-the-chrome-v4.0.5-webstore.zip`.
