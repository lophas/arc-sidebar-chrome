## Arc Side of the Chrome 4.0.3

*Spaces, tabs & a little harmony.*

- Keep a Space’s red indicator visible while additional tabs remain in its Chrome groups, even after every pinned tab is closed.
- Keep Fixed/Autohide mode, timeout, edge-trigger geometry and Favorite button size local to each device. Backup and restore continue to include them.
- Remove the legacy synchronized trigger entry while preserving existing local settings.

For an unpacked installation, download `arc-side-of-the-chrome-v4.0.3.zip`, replace the files in your existing folder and click **Reload** at `chrome://extensions`.
For a Chrome Web Store upload, use `arc-side-of-the-chrome-v4.0.3-webstore.zip`.
