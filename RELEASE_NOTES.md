## Arc Side of the Chrome 4.0.0

*Spaces, tabs & a little harmony.*

### A complete Space-oriented Chrome workspace

- Navigate by Space from either the sidebar or Chrome’s native tab bar. Matching group colors, order and focus keep both views in step.
- Keep global Favorites, persistent pinned links, folders and additional project tabs together, with drag-and-drop organization and practical close controls.
- Find saved links and open tabs across windows with ⌘K / Ctrl+K, including internal Chrome pages, or jump straight back to the previous tab.
- Continue compatible HTML5 videos in an automatic floating mini player and return them to the page when switching back.
- Personalize the whole sidebar with Space palettes, custom icons and adjustable Fixed/Autohide behavior, including precise edge trigger controls.
- Bring all your organization from Arc Browser, synchronize it with Chrome Sync, and preserve your layout and preferences in portable backups.
- Discover the extension through a completely rewritten README, a separate user guide and fresh screenshots of the current interface and native Chrome integration.

Download `arc-side-of-the-chrome-v4.0.0.zip`, replace the files in your existing extension folder and click **Reload** at `chrome://extensions`. Your saved organization and preferences are retained. The same ZIP contains the extension files for a Chrome Web Store upload.
