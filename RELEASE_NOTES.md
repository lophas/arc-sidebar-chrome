## Arc Side of the Chrome 4.1.1

*Spaces, tabs & a little harmony.*

- Use softly grey-tinted surfaces throughout light mode, including the sidebar background, Favorites, folders, search, dialogs and Options.
- Keep transparent white and black icons readable with a neutral backing and fine outline, preserving their original artwork and size.
- Dark mode retains its existing appearance.

For an unpacked installation, download `arc-side-of-the-chrome-v4.1.1.zip`, replace the files in your existing folder and click **Reload** at `chrome://extensions`.
For a Chrome Web Store upload, use `arc-side-of-the-chrome-v4.1.1-webstore.zip`.
