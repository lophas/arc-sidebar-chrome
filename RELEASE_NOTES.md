## Arc Side of the Chrome 3.1.5

*Spaces, tabs & a little harmony.*

- Return closed Favorite child tabs to their opener and follow Chrome tab Spaces
- Respect immediate manual focus changes and initialize saved tabs through the sidebar
- Exercise Favorite children through real webpage link clicks
- Check Space highlighting when the sidebar test view is visible

Download `arc-side-of-the-chrome-v3.1.5.zip`, replace the files in your existing extension folder and click **Reload** at `chrome://extensions`. Your saved organization and preferences are retained.
