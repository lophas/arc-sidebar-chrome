## Arc Side of the Chrome 3.1.13

*Spaces, tabs & a little harmony.*

- Horizontal Back/Forward swipe gestures over the sidebar no longer navigate its contents away.
- Vertical sidebar scrolling and navigation gestures over the webpage remain available.

Download `arc-side-of-the-chrome-v3.1.13.zip`, replace the files in your existing extension folder and click **Reload** at `chrome://extensions`. Your saved organization and preferences are retained.

[Full changelog](https://github.com/lophas/arc-sidebar-chrome/compare/v3.1.12...v3.1.13)
