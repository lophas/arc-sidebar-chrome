## Arc Side of the Chrome 3.1.14

*Spaces, tabs & a little harmony.*

- Adjust autohide timeout, edge trigger height, vertical position and width with sliders and a live preview in Options.
- Click outside the sidebar to hide it immediately. Dragging and releasing do not reopen it; unsaved editor fields are preserved.
- Edge trigger settings are included in Chrome Sync and backup/restore. Existing trigger defaults are retained.

Download `arc-side-of-the-chrome-v3.1.14.zip`, replace the files in your existing extension folder and click **Reload** at `chrome://extensions`. Your saved organization and preferences are retained.
