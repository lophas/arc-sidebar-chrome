## Arc Side of the Chrome 4.0.4

*Spaces, tabs & a little harmony.*

- Restore the yellow toolbar warning on already-open pages where Autohide is unavailable after an extension reload, update or browser startup, without requiring a mode switch.

For an unpacked installation, download `arc-side-of-the-chrome-v4.0.4.zip`, replace the files in your existing folder and click **Reload** at `chrome://extensions`.
For a Chrome Web Store upload, use `arc-side-of-the-chrome-v4.0.4-webstore.zip`.
