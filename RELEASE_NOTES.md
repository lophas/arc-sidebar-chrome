## Arc Side of the Chrome 4.1.0

*Spaces, tabs & a little harmony.*

- Give folders their own colors with ready-made shades, a custom color picker and a live preview.
- Keep each folder’s links together in a rounded container with a colored heading and matching background. Nested folders can use independent colors, in both light and dark appearances.
- Folder colors follow moves and reordering, and are included in Chrome Sync and backups. Older backups without color settings remain fully restorable.

For an unpacked installation, download `arc-side-of-the-chrome-v4.1.0.zip`, replace the files in your existing folder and click **Reload** at `chrome://extensions`.
For a Chrome Web Store upload, use `arc-side-of-the-chrome-v4.1.0-webstore.zip`.
